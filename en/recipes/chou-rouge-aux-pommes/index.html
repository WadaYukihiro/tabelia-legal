Chou Rouge aux PommesRed Cabbage with Apples

Difficulty★★☆☆☆ Prep30 min Cook1 hr 50 min Rest12 hr servings4

An Alsace-inspired side dish of red cabbage and apples braised with red wine and spices. Begin slowly with an overnight marinade and finish with the apples retaining their shape.

Ingredients (4 servings)

  • Red cabbageRemove the core and finely shred into 1–2 mm strips600 g
  • Tart applesPeel, core and cut into 8 wedges. Use tart apples that hold their shape, such as Jonathan300 g
  • OnionThinly slice100 g
  • Goose fatFor sautéing30 g
  • Red wine vinegarFor marinating and preserving the color60 ml
  • Red wineA dry wine with gentle tannins, such as Pinot Noir150 ml
  • Chicken stockBraising liquid100 ml
  • Cassonade (brown cane sugar)For balancing sweet and sour20 g
  • Redcurrant jellyAdds shine and acidity to finish30 g
  • Juniper berriesLightly crush with the flat of a knife6 pieces
  • ClovesStick into a piece of onion or put in a spice bag2 pieces
  • Cinnamon stickPut in the spice bag1 piece
  • Bay leafPut in the spice bag1 piece
  • Fine salt4 g for marinating and 2 g to finish6 g
  • Black pepperCoarsely grind2 g
  • Flat-leaf parsleyRoughly chop3 g

Method

125 min

Shred the cabbage and prepare aromatics and a spice bag

  • 1 Quarter 600 g red cabbage and cut away the tough central core.
  • 2 Place the quarters cut-side down and use a knife or mandoline to shred into 1–2 mm strips.
  • 3 Slice 100 g onion along the grain into 2 mm slices, but leave 1 larger piece uncut and stud it with 2 cloves.
  • 4 Lightly crush 6 juniper berries with the flat of a knife to crack their skins.
  • 5 Put the 6 crushed juniper berries, 1/2 cinnamon stick and 1 bay leaf in gauze or a tea filter and tie closed with kitchen string to make a spice bag.

Ready when

The cabbage should be evenly shredded and the spice bag and clove-studded onion ready.

212 hr

Marinate overnight in wine and vinegar to fix the ruby color

  • 1 Meanwhile(During marinating) keep the spice bag in the refrigerator alongside the bowl of cabbage.
  • 2 Put the 600 g shredded cabbage in a large bowl, sprinkle evenly with 4 g salt and massage lightly by hand.
  • 3 Pour 60 ml red wine vinegar and 150 ml red wine over all the cabbage and mix by lifting from the bottom with your hands for 2–3 minutes.
  • 4 Add 20 g brown sugar and 2 g black pepper and mix again. Press plastic wrap against the surface and refrigerate for at least 12 hours, from the previous evening to the next day.

Ready when

Proceed when the cabbage has relaxed and lost volume, with reddish-purple marinade collecting at the bottom of the bowl.

Its color should have changed from bluish purple to vivid reddish purple.

Caution

Do not marinate in aluminum containers, which are vulnerable to acid.

315 min

Sauté the onion in goose fat and coat the cabbage

  • 1 Melt 30 g goose fat completely in a heavy cast-iron casserole over medium heat.
  • 2 Add the sliced onion and sauté over medium heat for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally with a wooden spoon, until translucent without browning.
  • 3 Drain the marinated cabbage in a colander, catching and reserving the marinade in a bowl.
  • 4 Add the drained cabbage to the pot and sauté over medium heat for 5 minutes, turning with a wooden spoon until coated in fat and lightly softened.

Ready when

Proceed when the onion is translucent and all the cabbage is glossy and reduced to about 2/3 of its volume in the pot.

450 min

Braise the cabbage with spices for 45 minutes

  • 1 Meanwhile(During the last 10 minutes of braising) peel and core the apples and cut the net 300 g into 8 wedges.
  • 2 Pour all the reserved marinade and 100 ml chicken stock into the pot. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat and boil for 2 minutes to drive off the smell of alcohol.
  • 3 Bury the clove-studded onion and spice bag in the center of the pot.
  • 4 Cover, lower the heat and simmer for 45 minutes with the liquid surface barely trembling.

Ready when

Proceed when the cabbage has softened and the firm parts are less hard.

Tips

If the liquid is close to drying out, add 50 ml boiling water.

530 min

Add the apples and cook without losing their shape

  • 1 Spread the apples over the cabbage, gently push them down and cover.
  • 2 Simmer over low heat for 25–30 minutes, turning once from the bottom without breaking the apples.

Ready when

Proceed when the cabbage is tender and a skewer passes through the apples while their shape remains intact.

68 min

Remove the spices and reduce the liquid

  • 1 Use tongs to remove and discard the spice bag and clove-studded onion.
  • 2 Uncover, increase to medium heat and reduce excess liquid for 5–8 minutes, stirring from the bottom with a wooden spoon.

Ready when

Proceed when a track drawn along the base with the spoon remains for 2–3 seconds instead of filling immediately.

The liquid should cling to the cabbage rather than run out when the pot is tilted.

76 min

Glaze with redcurrant jelly and serve with parsley

  • 1 Roughly chop 3 g parsley.
  • 2 Lower the heat, add 30 g redcurrant jelly and stir with a wooden spoon for 2 minutes to melt it throughout.
  • 3 Sprinkle with 2 g salt, stir briefly and taste.
  • 4 Pile into a serving dish and scatter the 3 g roughly chopped flat-leaf parsley over the surface.

Ready when

It is ready when the jelly is fully dissolved, everything is glossy and sweetness and acidity are balanced.

About this dish

Cabbage and apples suit the tables of eastern France, including Alsace. Braised red cabbage is a side dish that supports meat dishes from autumn through winter.

A small helping beside the main course adds color to a family meal. The choice of alcohol and spices varies by locality and household; this version uses red wine.

Chef Authen’s tip

The vegetable called purple cabbage in Japanese is named red cabbage in French. Even in the way colors are perceived, dish names preserve the language of their locality.
