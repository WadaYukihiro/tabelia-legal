Authentic Peperonata

Difficulty★☆☆☆☆ Prep10 min Cook30 min servings4

Bell peppers, tomato and onion slowly cooked in olive oil — a Southern Italian side dish that pairs equally well with meat or fish, and tastes just as good cold.

IngredientsMethod

Ingredients (4 servings)

  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Onion1
  • Fresh basil5
  • Bell peppers (red and yellow)2
  • Garlic1 clove
  • Canned peeled tomatoes200 g

Method

1

Prepare the vegetables

Remove the seeds and pith from the bell peppers and slice into 1.5cm strips. Thinly slice the onion.

2

Soften the onion

Heat 3 tablespoons olive oil over medium heat, add the onion and crushed garlic, and cook for 3 minutes.

3

Simmer the peppers

Add the peppers and cook for a further 5 minutes. Crush in the canned tomatoes by hand, cover and simmer over low heat for 20 minutes.

4

Finish with basil

Season with salt, remove from the heat and tear in the basil. Good served warm or at room temperature.
