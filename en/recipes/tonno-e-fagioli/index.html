About this dish

Tonno e fagioli is among the most unpretentious and honest dishes of Tuscan home cooking. With good canned tuna and white cannellini beans, it requires no special technique. It embodies rural resourcefulness and is also said to have originated when fishermen’s salted tuna met farmers’ beans.

In Tuscany, “fagioli” means cannellini beans. Their pleasantly yielding texture and mild flavour readily take on the character of olive oil. Use good-quality tuna preserved in oil and drain off only a little excess oil to retain the tuna’s own savouriness.

Chef Authen’s tip

Despite using canned tuna, this dish reflects the importance Italians place on tuna quality. Belly meat, or ventresca, preserved in oil has a depth of rich savouriness far beyond ordinary canned tuna. Being selective about the brand, even for a canned ingredient, helps create an authentic plate.
