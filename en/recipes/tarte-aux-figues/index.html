Tarte aux FiguesFig and Almond Tart

Difficulty★★★★☆ Prep35 min Cook1 hr 5 min Rest2 hr servings6

An autumn tart of sweet pastry filled with almond cream and baked figs. The cream catches the fruit juices, bringing crisp baked pastry and a moist center together.

IngredientsMethodAbout this dish

Ingredients (6 servings)

  • Cake flour150 g for pastry, 10 g for dusting.160 g
  • Unsalted butter for pastry75 g
  • Icing sugar40 g
  • Beaten egg for pastry30 g
  • Salt1 g
  • Fresh figs450 g
  • Ground almonds60 g
  • Unsalted butter for almond cream60 g
  • Caster sugar60 g
  • Beaten egg for almond cream60 g

Method

110 min

Make pâte sucrée

  • 1 Cream 75 g softened pastry butter with 40 g icing sugar and 1 g salt in a bowl.
  • 2 Mix in 30 g beaten egg in 2 additions, sift in 150 g flour and bring together with cutting motions of a spatula.
  • 3 Form a 2 cm thick disc and wrap.

Ready when

Continue when no dry flour remains and it has not become sticky through overworking.

21 hr

Chill and rest

  • 1 Refrigerate the wrapped dough for 1 hour.

Ready when

Continue when chilled and firm, indenting slightly under a gentle finger press.

340 min

Line the tart tin

  • 1 MeanwhileWhile the lined dough chills, preheat the oven to 170°C.
  • 2 Lightly dust the worktop with 10 g flour and roll dough to 3 mm thick, about 27 cm across.
  • 3 Line a tart tin 22 cm across and about 2 cm high, fitting into corners and trimming excess.
  • 4 Prick the base and chill for 30 minutes. Wrap and freeze the trimmings.

Ready when

Continue when base and sides are similar in thickness, with no large gaps at corners.

420 min

Blind-bake the shell

  • 1 Line with parchment and weights and bake at 170°C for 15 minutes.
  • 2 Remove weights and paper, bake another 5 minutes and place on a rack.

Ready when

Continue when the base is dry and edges pale golden.

515 min

Mix almond cream

  • 1 Cream 60 g softened filling butter with 60 g sugar using a spatula.
  • 2 Mix in 60 g beaten egg in 3 additions, then 60 g ground almonds until uniform.
  • 3 Wash and dry 450 g figs, trim hard stems and cut lengthwise into 4.

Ready when

Continue when the crème d'amande, almond cream, is smooth without separated fat.

Caution

Overwhipping makes it puff and crack during baking.

640 min

Arrange figs and bake

  • 1 Spread almond cream evenly in the shell and arrange figs radially, cut-side up, pressing lightly in.
  • 2 Bake at 170°C for 35–40 minutes and transfer the tin to a rack.

Ready when

It is done when the cream is golden and a skewer in a fruit-free area carries no raw cream.

Caution

Fruit juice can coat the skewer; judge doneness in the cream itself.

71 hr

Cool on a rack

  • 1 Cool in the tin on a rack for 1 hour.

Ready when

Continue when the tin is no longer hot and the cream's center has settled.

85 min

Slice the fig tart

  • 1 Unmold, cut into 6 and plate.

Ready when

It is ready when the shell holds and fruit and cream remain together.

About this dish

French fruit tarts bring market seasons to the table. In late summer and autumn, when figs appear, a tart lined with purple fruit feels right.

Fruit evoking the south is portioned for family dessert or afternoon gatherings. Fig tarts take many forms; this version pairs them with almond cream.

Chef Authen’s tip

The name tarte aux figues does not specify the cream inside. Some versions bake the fruit and others add it fresh at the end. Here the figs are baked into the tart.
