WHY TABELIA

We don’t simplify recipes. We make them possible.

Tabelia starts from each dish as it is actually cooked in its place of origin — the traditional ingredients, the unhurried steps, the judgment calls that decide how the dish turns out. Nothing essential is cut for convenience.

The Tabelia app then adapts each recipe to your kitchen — your servings, your ingredients, your schedule — and is honest about what every substitution changes in the finished dish.

  1. 01
    Authentic technique

    Nothing is cut. Every judgment call that shapes the finished dish is written down.

  2. 02
    Doneness you can see

    Temperatures, timings and visual cues tell you exactly when a step is done — not just what to do.

  3. 03
    Made for your kitchen

    Ingredients, servings and timing, adapted to what you actually have.
