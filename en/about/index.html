ABOUT TABELIA

About Tabelia

Tabelia is a recipe service for cooking authentic Italian dishes in your own kitchen. Rather than trimming recipes for speed or convenience, we start from the ingredients and methods passed down where each dish comes from, then convert them into a form you can actually cook. Our motto is “For cooks who won’t compromise.” — a service for people who take the time to get a dish right.

WHY TABELIA

We don’t simplify recipes. We make them possible.

Tabelia starts from each dish as it is actually cooked in Italy — the traditional ingredients, the unhurried steps, the judgment calls that decide how the dish turns out. Nothing essential is cut for convenience.

The Tabelia app then adapts each recipe to your kitchen — your servings, your ingredients, your schedule — and is honest about what every substitution changes in the finished dish.

AUTHENTICITY SCORE

What is the Authenticity Score?

The Authenticity Score is Tabelia’s own 0–100 measure of how closely a dish stays true to its original form. Cook the standard recipe exactly and the score is 100. Choose a substitution and the score drops according to its impact, with a clear explanation of what changes in flavour, texture and technique.

Swap guanciale for bacon in a carbonara, for instance, and you gain a smokier edge but lose the sweetness of guanciale’s rendered fat. Tabelia never rules out a substitution — it simply always tells you, in numbers and in words, what that choice changes.
